Our Approach

Ownership done right, so flying stays simple.

We invest in proven training aircraft and partner with schools that need reliable airplanes without the capital outlay. Clear agreements, well-funded maintenance reserves, and an owner who actually answers the phone.

Well-maintained aircraft

Our fleet is acquired, insured, and maintained to keep airplanes available and on the schedule — funded reserves for engine, prop, and unscheduled maintenance.

Transparent terms

Straightforward leaseback agreements with clear revenue splits and responsibilities. No surprises, no fine print working against the school.

Owner-first partnership

We align incentives so a well-flown, well-cared-for airplane is a win for everyone. You focus on training and operations; we focus on the asset.
